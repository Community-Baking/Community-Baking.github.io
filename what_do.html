Community Baking

What we do on Saturdays:

  • Make dough
  • Oil pans and transfer dough to pans
  • Let it rise (1 hr)
  • Add toppings
  • Bake (25 min)
  • Cut into servings, wrap and label
  • Do dishes and clean up
  • One or two people deliver the bread to the WooFridge
