Community Baking

What we do on Saturdays:

  • Measure and mix ingredients for dough
  • Oil pans and transfer dough to pans
  • Let dough rise
  • Add toppings
  • Bake
  • Stmap labels on boxes, and fold boxes
  • Cut bread into servings, and box it up
  • Do dishes and clean up
  • One or two people deliver the bread to the WooFridge. Find more details here: Where the Bread Goes
