Community Baking

What to Bring

You don't have to bring anything!

If you want to, bring any of the following:

  • Toppings for our bread: cheese, herbs, tomatoes, onions, or anything else you like
  • Donations for the WooFridges: Bottled water is the most commonly requested item. Find more info about donations here: https://www.woofridge.org/ways-to-help
  • Snacks and drinks: For yourself or to share with other bakers
  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil: We use about 1 liter per baking day
  • Flour "Bread flour" is preferred, but any flour will work. We often use organic flour, but that's not a requirement. We typically use 10-20 lbs per baking day.
